I spent a few days in Naples, including a side trip to Pompeii. Pompeii is reasonably short train ride from Naples, and the site has its own train stop.

The site of Pompeii is enmormous - not surprisingly, as it an entire ruined city that stretches block after block.

Its possible to get a bit lost, as signage is limited. I somewhat foolishly didnt pick up a paper map. However, following groups of people was a good way to ensure I checked out some of the more famous structures.
