In 2017, I visited the very unique city of Guanajuato. This City is very striking, with colourful buildings built on the sides of a valley. 

The City developed alongside important silver mines, including one known as La Valenciana. This area was a major centre of silver extraction during the colonial period, and Guanajuato was a site of great wealth.

You can tour parts of this mine - a rather intense and claustrophic experience. Beside this mine is a spectactular church - one of the great Baroque churches of Mexico.

This city was the site of a massacre of spanish inhabitants during the abortive 1810 war of independence led by Hidalgo. This site of the massacre, the Alhondiga, is still marked by bullet holes, and is now a rather haunting museum.

Guanajuato is a university city nowadays, and bustling with young people. I spent a week studying Spanish here, while boarding with a local family.
