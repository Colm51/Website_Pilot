In 2022, I visited the classic diving destination of Utila - an Island off the coast of Honduras.

Long known as an affordable destination for people wanting to learn how to dive, Utila is much lower-key than the nearby Island of Roatan, which attracts wealthier expats and tourists.

Utila remains a destination of choice for younger, budget-conscious travellers. 

The permanent population of the Island are afro-carribean, as opposed to the spanish / indigenous mestizos who dominate the mainland.

The water conditions are great for divers - warm water, little current - and a spectacular coral reef where one sees all manner of tropical sealife.
