  <p>The dashboard is organized around three related ideas: <strong>Collection</strong> covers everything in the music library; <strong>Curation</strong> covers songs I manually selected into one or more playlists while listening; and <strong>Listening</strong> is represented separately by Play Count.</p>

  <h2>What the dashboard explores</h2>
  <p>It brings together collection and listening summaries, collection versus curation, playlist statistics, music by year, most-played songs, and genre patterns.</p>
